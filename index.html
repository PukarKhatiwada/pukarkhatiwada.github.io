About me

A frontend developer who likes doing things properly.

Hey, I'm Pukar Khatiwada — a frontend web developer living in Ilam, a small hill town in eastern Nepal better known for its tea gardens than its tech scene. Mornings here stay foggy till about ten, and honestly not a lot happens, which is probably why I've got the patience to sit with a layout until it's actually right instead of shipping "good enough."

I started out with plain HTML and CSS years ago and never really felt the need to hide behind a framework. I'll use one when a project genuinely calls for it — React, whatever fits — but I like knowing exactly what's happening in the code I ship, and I think it makes me better at fixing things when they break.

These days I build websites for small businesses, individuals, and the occasional startup — mostly in Nepal, sometimes further out. If you want a site that loads fast, works on your customer's cheap phone, and doesn't look like everyone else's template, that's the kind of project I like taking on.

Good to know

Frequently asked.

What do you actually build with?+

Hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No page builders. I'll bring in a framework if a project genuinely calls for one, but it's not the default.

Do you take on small projects?+

Yes — landing pages and single-page sites are welcome, not just the bigger stuff.

How long does a build take?+

Depends on the scope, but most small sites go from brief to live in a couple of weeks once I have the content.

What happens after launch?+

I'm still around for fixes and small updates. A site shouldn't start rotting the day it goes live.
